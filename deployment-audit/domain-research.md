Treat every price below as the extension’s advertised price, not a confirmed quote. A premium name can cost more even when the registry says it is unregistered.

WHOIS privacy: Hostinger’s domains page says privacy protection is included at no extra cost on extensions that support it. `.ai` and `.si` are named as exceptions. `.com` support for free privacy is therefore the general rule on that page, and the Hindi `.com` page also lists privacy as free. Confirm the toggle is on before paying.

Auto-renewal is on by default and is charged 27 days before expiry. Domains are not part of the 30-day hosting money-back guarantee. `.com` refunds, if offered, are described as a 96-hour window. Country-code refunds are generally excluded. Read the checkout text.

ICANN requires the contact email to be confirmed within 15 days.

## Recommendation

1. At checkout, search `chiranshfoods.com` on the Hostinger domain page and read the due-today figure.
2. If the hosting plan’s “domain free for 1 year” includes this `.com`, use that offer instead of paying the 3-year promo as well. The interaction of the two offers was **Not Verified**.
3. If the cart price is the standard promo or the normal ₹1,499 year, buy `chiranshfoods.com`.
4. If the cart shows a large premium, stop and decide. `chiranshfoods.in` is the backup name and was available the same evening.
5. Optionally add `chiranshfoods.in` in a second step so the Indian spelling is yours. Redirect it to the canonical host. Do not run a second copy of the app on it (F-05).

## Brand notes

- Prefer the full words “chiransh foods” so the name matches speech.
- `.com` is the better primary address for a food brand that may be shared on WhatsApp and Instagram.
- `.in` tells an Ahmedabad customer the business is Indian. It is a good alias, and a slightly worse primary, because some people will still type `.com`.
- Hyphens and alternate spellings (`chiranash`, `chiransh-foods`) were not checked. Avoid them if the unhyphenated `.com` can be bought.

## What this report does not do

It does not reserve a domain. It does not confirm trademark clearance. A matching trade name or FSSAI record was not searched. That legal check is yours if you want it.
